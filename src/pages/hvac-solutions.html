Annual Maintenance Contracts

Comprehensive & Non-Comprehensive AMC

We structure AMC coverage around how a facility actually operates, with two standard formats:

Comprehensive AMC

Scheduled preventive maintenance visits combined with repair and breakdown coverage, so upkeep and unplanned issues are handled under one contract.

  • • Scheduled preventive maintenance
  • • Breakdown & repair response included
  • • Priority scheduling for site visits

Non-Comprehensive AMC

Scheduled preventive maintenance visits to keep systems running efficiently, with repair work quoted and carried out separately as needed.

  • • Scheduled preventive maintenance
  • • Repairs quoted separately as required
  • • Lower fixed annual cost
